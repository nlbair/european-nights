HOW TO PLAY

The Goal

Spin 11 times to build a squad from 11 different Champions League clubs and simulate your UCL campaign.

Each Spin

You get a random club and season. Pick 1 player from that pool, place them on the pitch, then the next spin happens automatically.

Positions

GKs go in goal, DEFs in the back 4, MIDs in the middle 3, FWDs in the front 3. Strict — no cross-position drafting.

The Simulation

Your team's average rating determines your UCL fate. Elite squads win the trophy. Results range from Group Stage exit to Champion.

Tips

A rating above 7.35 gives you a shot at the title. Watch for Man City 23/24, PSG 17/18, and Barcelona 15/16 — the strongest pools in the game.
