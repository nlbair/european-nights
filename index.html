HOW TO PLAY

The Goal

Draft 11 players from a Champions League club's best season and simulate your UCL campaign. The catch: you don't get to choose your club.

Spinning

Hit Spin to get a random club and season combo — for example Real Madrid 2017/18 or Liverpool 2018/19. You have 2 skips if you hate what you landed on.

Drafting

Choose 11 players: 1 GK, 4 DEF, 3 MID, and 3 FWD. Players are sorted by rating. Use position filters to narrow the list. Click a player, place them on the pitch, then assign a shirt number.

The Simulation

Your team's average rating determines your UCL fate. Elite squads win the trophy. Weaker squads exit early. Results range from Group Stage exit to Champion.

Tips

A rating above 7.35 gives you a shot at the title. Watch for Man City 23/24, PSG 17/18, and Barcelona 15/16 — the strongest pools in the game.
